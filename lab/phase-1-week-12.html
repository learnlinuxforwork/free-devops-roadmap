1Full practice exam(s); review every missed domain

Run at least one more full practice exam. For every missed question, don't just read the correct answer — go back to the actual AWS console or CLI and reproduce the scenario the question was testing. Recognition on a multiple-choice exam is not the same skill as knowing the service.

A quick self-check across the four domains
Cloud Concepts:              Can you explain elasticity, agility, and the 6 benefits of cloud?
Security and Compliance:      Shared responsibility, IAM, root account best practices?
Cloud Technology & Services:  Core compute/storage/database/networking services and their fit?
Billing and Pricing:          Free tier, Support plans, Cost Explorer, Budgets, pricing models?

2Sit the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) exam

Booking checklist
1. Create/sign in to your AWS Certification account: aws.training
2. Schedule via Pearson VUE, either a test center or online proctored
3. Online proctored needs: private room, valid photo ID, webcam, stable connection
4. Arrive/log in 30 minutes early -- check-in and ID verification take time
5. The exam is 90 minutes, 65 questions, scored 100-1000, passing score is 700
Phase 1 exit criteria

You've deployed a static site behind CloudFront, built a VPC by hand and can explain every piece of it, decoupled two scripts with SQS, and passed (or are ready to pass) CLF-C02. Phase 2 assumes this foundation is solid — it goes deeper into the same services rather than introducing entirely new ones.
