1The Well-Architected Framework's 6 pillars

The six pillars — know what each one actually asks
Operational Excellence   Run and monitor systems, improve processes continuously
Security                 Protect data, systems, and assets; least privilege by default
Reliability               Recover from failure automatically; scale to meet demand
Performance Efficiency    Use resources efficiently; keep up as demand and tech evolve
Cost Optimization         Avoid unneeded spend; understand where money goes
Sustainability            Minimize environmental impact of running workloads
Checkpoint

For your capstone VPC from Week 9, name one concrete decision you made that maps to Reliability (hint: Multi-AZ) and one that maps to Cost Optimization (hint: NAT gateway is billed hourly plus per-GB — is one enough, or do you need one per AZ?).

2Billing dashboards, Cost Explorer, budgets and alerts

Enable and query Cost Explorer
aws ce get-cost-and-usage \
  --time-period Start=2026-07-01,End=2026-07-25 \
  --granularity DAILY \
  --metrics "UnblendedCost" \
  --group-by Type=DIMENSION,Key=SERVICE
A budget with an alert threshold
aws budgets create-budget --account-id <ACCOUNT_ID> \
  --budget '{
    "BudgetName": "phase1-cap",
    "BudgetLimit": {"Amount": "25", "Unit": "USD"},
    "TimeUnit": "MONTHLY", "BudgetType": "COST"
  }' \
  --notifications-with-subscribers '[{
    "Notification": {"NotificationType": "ACTUAL", "ComparisonOperator": "GREATER_THAN",
                      "Threshold": 80, "ThresholdType": "PERCENTAGE"},
    "Subscribers": [{"SubscriptionType": "EMAIL", "Address": "you@example.com"}]
  }]'
Common trap

NAT gateways, unattached EBS volumes, and idle RDS instances are the three most common sources of surprise AWS bills for learners. Tear down anything from a prior week you're not actively using.

3Practice exam #1 for Cloud Practitioner; log your weak areas

Take a full-length CLF-C02 practice exam under real conditions — timed, no notes. Score yourself against the four exam domains: Cloud Concepts, Security and Compliance, Cloud Technology and Services, Billing and Pricing.

Add this to the break/fix log you've been keeping since Phase 0
## 2026-07-25 -- CLF-C02 practice exam #1 -- Score: 68%

### Weak domain: Billing and Pricing
- Missed: Savings Plans vs Reserved Instances tradeoffs
- Missed: which services have a free tier vs which don't
- Action: re-read AWS pricing pages for EC2, S3, RDS this week
Checkpoint

Score at or above 80% before moving to Week 12 — if you're below that, spend extra time on the weakest domain rather than pushing forward on schedule.
