1Write a runbook for your capstone app (common failure modes + remediation)

A runbook entry — specific enough that someone else could follow it at 3am
## Symptom: 5xx error rate above 5% for 5+ minutes

### Likely causes, in order of probability
1. Bad deploy -- check: kubectl rollout history deployment/roadmap-api -n roadmap
   Fix: kubectl rollout undo deployment/roadmap-api -n roadmap
2. Database connection exhaustion -- check: RDS CloudWatch DatabaseConnections metric
   Fix: scale connection pool down, or aws rds modify-db-instance --max-connections
3. Downstream dependency down -- check: X-Ray service map for red edges
   Fix: confirm the dependency's own status, enable circuit breaker if not already on

### Escalation
If unresolved in 15 minutes, page the secondary on-call via the process in docs/oncall.md

Write one runbook entry per failure mode you've actually caused this roadmap — the RDS failover from Week 10, the SELinux denial pattern from Phase 0, the broken deploy from Week 38's rollback drill. Real incidents you've lived through make far better runbooks than hypothetical ones.

2Learn SLOs/SLIs/error budgets; define one for the capstone app

The vocabulary
SLI (indicator)   A measured metric, e.g. "% of requests completing under 300ms"
SLO (objective)   A target for that SLI, e.g. "99.5% of requests under 300ms, over 30 days"
Error budget       The allowed failure margin: 100% - SLO = 0.5% of requests can miss target
                    That 0.5% is a budget you can spend on risk -- deploys, experiments, etc.
Turn Week 42's Grafana panels into an SLO definition
SLI: sum(rate(http_requests_total{status!~"5.."}[30d])) / sum(rate(http_requests_total[30d]))
SLO: 99.5% over a rolling 30-day window
Error budget: 0.5% of 30 days = ~3.6 hours of full downtime equivalent per month
Checkpoint

Once you have a real error budget number, connect it to a policy: 'if the error budget is exhausted this month, we freeze non-critical deploys until it recovers.' That policy — not the dashboard — is the actual point of defining an SLO.
